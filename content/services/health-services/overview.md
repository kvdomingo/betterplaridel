# Overview

## Vision

By the year 2040, Plaridelenos will be able to attain a level of health that
will enable them to lead a socially, economically productive quality of life.

## Mission

Assure sustained delivery of basic services by providing preventive, curative,
and rehabilitative aspects of Primary Health Care.
